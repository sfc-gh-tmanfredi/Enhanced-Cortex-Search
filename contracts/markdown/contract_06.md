**B.2** Severity 1 means a complete loss of production service or any incident affecting Covered Data.

**B.3** Response times: Severity 1 — 15 minutes; Severity 2 — 2 hours; Severity 3 — 1 Business Day.

**B.4** Service Credit: 5% of the monthly monthly rent for each 0.1% below the availability target.

**B.5** Service Credits shall not exceed 15% of the monthly monthly rent in any month.

**B.6** "Chronic SLA Failure" means failure to meet the availability target in (a) two (2) consecutive months or (b) any three (3) months within a rolling twelve-month period.
