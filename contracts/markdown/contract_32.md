# SAAS SUBSCRIPTION AGREEMENT

This SaaS Subscription Agreement (the "Agreement") is entered into as of March 1, 2024 (the "Effective Date") by and between Alpine Ski Holdings Ltd ("Vendor") and Munson's Pickles Inc. ("Subscriber") (each a "Party" and collectively the "Parties").

**RECITALS**

WHEREAS, Vendor is in the business of providing the services described herein; and

WHEREAS, Subscriber wishes to obtain such services on the terms set forth below;

## Section 1. Definitions

**1.1** "Business Day" means any day other than a Saturday, Sunday or public holiday in the place of performance.

**1.2** "Covered Data" means Personal Data, and any Subscriber Data designated as Restricted under Exhibit C. For clarity, Covered Data excludes De-identified Data.

**1.3** "Critical Incident" means a Severity 1 incident as described in Exhibit B, paragraph B.2.

**1.4** "Fees" means the amounts set out in Exhibit A, as adjusted under Section 7.4.

**1.5** "Liability Cap" means the amount determined under Section 13.2.

**1.6** "Special Damages Cap" means three (3) times the Liability Cap.

**1.7** "Termination for Convenience Fee" means the fee described in Exhibit A, paragraph A.6.
